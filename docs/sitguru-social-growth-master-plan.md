# SitGuru Social Media & Community Growth Master Plan

**Position:** Social Media & Community Growth Manager  
**Principle:** Grow SitGuru’s local marketplace. Followers are not the scoreboard. Registrations and first bookings are.

Do not build a marketing department, a Hootsuite clone, or a pile of SaaS tools. Hire one person, give them a simple secure workspace, and judge them on Pet Parents, Gurus, and bookings.

---

## 1. The problem

SitGuru already has things to talk about: Pet Parents, Gurus, Ambassadors, events, partners, PawPerks, PawReport, referrals, community, local discovery, real Guru profiles, and the founder story.

The gap is not content. The gap is someone who **consistently tells the story, distributes it, engages people, and turns attention into signups**.

Their job:

> Content → Attention → SitGuru → Signup → Booking

Not: “Post something on Instagram every day.”

## 3. What they own

**Do**

- Run Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok first.
- Learn SitGuru well enough to explain Pet Parents, Gurus, Ambassadors, PawPerks, PawReport, events, and partners.
- Create Reels, short videos, posts, Stories, and local community content.
- Join relevant Facebook groups and actually help. Do not dump ads.
- Promote individual Gurus and their stories.
- Promote local pet events and SitGuru partners.
- Reach out to local pet influencers, rescues, groomers, trainers, vets, and businesses.
- Reply to comments and DMs.
- Send people to **specific signup pages**, not just the homepage.
- Track Pet Parent and Guru registrations from social.
- Send a short Friday report.

**Do not measure them on** Instagram going from 300 to 1,000 followers.

**Do measure them on** people joining and using SitGuru.

## 5. Hiring funnel

Do not interview 25 people.

| Stage | Count | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| Applicants | 20–30 | Collect |
| Portfolio review | 10 | Look at Reels, writing, local work |
| Paid challenge | 5 | SitGuru.com + $500 / 30 days prompt |
| Interviews | 3 | Questions below |
| Paid test | 1–2 | $75–$150 sample pack |
| 30-day hire | 1 | Contractor trial |

### The one challenge that matters

Give them SitGuru.com and say:

> You have $500 and 30 days. Our biggest problem is getting Pet Parents in Bucks County and the Lehigh Valley to discover SitGuru and create accounts. Walk me through exactly what you would do.

**Weak answer:** hashtags, posting schedules, brand awareness.  
**Strong answer:** Facebook groups, Guru videos, UGC, events, micro-influencers, referral codes, tracking links, CTAs, retargeting, Reels, tests, and **registrations**.

### Interview questions

1. Explain SitGuru as if I am a Pet Parent who has never heard of it.
2. What would your first SitGuru Reel be?
3. What are five posts you would make in week one?
4. How would you grow Pet Parents in one county?
5. How would you promote a new Guru?
6. How would you use SitGuru events?
7. How would you approach local pet businesses?
8. What would you do in Facebook groups?
9. How would you find local pet influencers?
10. What do you need from me every week?
11. How would you prove your work is helping SitGuru?

### Paid test ($75–$150)

Give: website, logo, one Guru, one event, one partner, brand direction.

Ask for:

- 1 Reel concept
- 1 Pet Parent post
- 1 Guru spotlight
- 1 local growth idea
- 1 seven-day content calendar

Judge creativity, SitGuru understanding, writing, visuals, strategy, speed, and communication.

Offer: **30-day paid contractor trial with a chance to continue based on performance.** No six-month promise.

## 7. Tool stack

Start with **six things**. Do not buy Hootsuite, HubSpot, Sprout, Monday, Asana, Later, and five AI tools on day one.

| Tool | Job | When |
|---|---|---|
| SitGuru Admin | Campaigns, approved Gurus/events/partners, tracking links, signup analytics | Day 1, required |

**Canva vs CapCut:** Canva = graphics. CapCut = video.  
**Ownership:** You own every account. They get employee access. Never share personal Facebook/TikTok/Canva passwords. If they leave, remove access.

### Canva workspace

Create a **SitGuru Canva team**, not your personal account. Brand kit: logos, fonts, `#0D5C3A`, Rogue, approved photos.

Templates to load before they start:

- Pet Parent: Why SitGuru, Find a Guru, pet-care tip, testimonial, PawPerks
- Guru: Meet Your Guru, Guru Spotlight, service highlight
- Events: This Weekend, upcoming pet events
- Partners: Partner Spotlight, local business
- Covers: Stories, Reel covers, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn

They should open “Meet Your Local Guru,” swap photo and details, and publish.

### Workflow once hired

```
SitGuru Admin (“Promote Kyra”)
  → Canva template
  → CapCut if video
  → copy campaign link
  → Meta / TikTok (later Buffer)
  → publish
  → SitGuru records clicks

```

### Email: keep two worlds apart

**SitGuru keeps (never hand to marketing):** password reset, booking confirmation, payment, account security, vetting, system alerts.

**Manager can later own in Mailchimp:** welcome series, Meet Gurus near you, events, PawPerks, refer a friend, haven’t booked yet, new features.

Example Mailchimp path later:

Welcome → Meet Gurus near you → Events near you → Finish pet profile → First booking → Refer a Pet Parent

## 8. You still make the raw content

15–30 minutes a week. They tell you the three clips. Example:

1. Why I built SitGuru  
2. Why SitGuru is not just another pet-sitting website  
3. Three things every Pet Parent should ask before hiring a sitter  

You record on your phone. They edit, caption, hook, cut, add music, post, and repurpose. One conversation can become five to ten pieces.

If they are remote, you or a Guru send event footage. They turn it into 1 Reel + 3 Stories + Facebook + TikTok + Guru spotlight.

## 9. Content system

**Quality over volume at first.** About 4–5 primary pieces per week, plus Stories and community replies.

| Day | Type |
|---|---|
| Monday | Guru Spotlight |
| Tuesday | Pet Parent educational / relatable |
| Wednesday | Community / event |
| Thursday | Founder or feature video |
| Friday | CTA: Find Care, PawPerks, or referral |

**Pillars** (so the feed is not “Join SitGuru” on repeat): Pets, Trust, Gurus, Pet Parents, Community, Education, SitGuru features, Founder, Referrals.

**Facebook groups:** Participate. Answer questions. Help people find care. Do not spam.

**Micro-influencers:** Local dog/cat accounts, rescues, trainers, groomers, vets, pet photographers, dog-friendly businesses. A 4,000-follower local dog account beats a 500,000-follower national celebrity.

**UGC:** Pet photos, Guru videos, walks, events, testimonials. Ask for permission. Use marketing-consent Gurus only.

## 10. How you know it is working

Scenario A: 20,000 Instagram followers, 7 Pet Parents. **Lose.**  
Scenario B: 2,300 followers, 183 Pet Parents. **Win.**

### Eight KPIs

1. Pet Parent registrations (most important at first)  
2. Guru registrations  
3. Social → SitGuru visits  
4. Signup conversion  
5. Referral registrations  
6. First bookings from social (as soon as you can attribute)  
7. Shares and saves  
8. DMs and real conversations  

Also track **density**, not just national totals: Bucks, Montgomery, Lehigh, Northampton, etc.

### Friday report (one page)

- Posts / Reels / Stories  
- Reach  
- SitGuru visitors  
- Pet Parents + / Gurus + / Referrals +  
- Best campaign / worst campaign  
- Next week (3 bullets)

### Your time

- Monday: 15-minute priorities  
- Midweek: approve if needed  
- Friday: numbers  

Do not rewrite every caption.

## 12. SitGuru Admin: build small, later

Do this in Cursor **when you are ready to implement**, not as a giant product. Extend existing Admin, auth, Guru, Event, Partner, referral, and analytics. Mobile-first.

### Role

`SOCIAL_COMMUNITY_MANAGER` (display: Social & Community Manager)

**Can:** Growth home, campaigns, marketing content, media, public Guru profiles, events, partner marketing copy, aggregated growth, referral summaries.

**Cannot:** Stripe, PayPal, payouts, banks, cards, background checks, IDs, passwords, API keys, Supabase, database, security, user delete, private messages, private PawReports, legal admin.

Show county-level counts, not “John Smith, 123 Main St, card, booking history.”

Hide in the UI **and** reject on the API. Both.

### Menu (Growth only)

Home · Create · Campaigns · Content · Gurus · Events · Partners · Media · Analytics

Phone bar: Home · Create · Campaigns · Media · More, plus a large **+ Create**.

Create choices: Guru · Event · Partner · PawPerks · Pet Parent · SitGuru Feature · General Post.

Pull **marketing-safe** fields only. Add Guru consent: *Allow SitGuru to feature my public profile in social and community marketing.* Filter “Approved for Promotion.”

### Campaign tracking (the one technical feature that matters)

Every real campaign gets a tracking URL, not bare sitguru.com.

Behind the scenes: campaign name, channel, post, owner, market, clicks, signups, bookings.

Funnel to architect toward: View → Click → Visit → Signup start → Signup complete → Role → First booking → Repeat.

Day 1 does not need every step perfect.

### Approval

Draft → Submit → Jason approves / requests changes → They publish. Drop approval for routine posts once you trust them.

### Do not build yet

Direct Instagram/Facebook/TikTok publishing APIs, social listening, AI marketing agents, influencer marketplace, Mailchimp clone, Hootsuite clone, giant CRM, advanced attribution.

They create in SitGuru, design in Canva/CapCut, publish in Meta/TikTok, SitGuru measures conversion.

### Cursor phases (when you say build)

**A — Security:** role, permissions, Team page, basic audit log.  
**B — Growth:** Growth Home, campaigns, tracking URLs, conversion dashboard.  
**C — Content:** Create flows, Guru/Event/Partner starters, media, draft/approved.

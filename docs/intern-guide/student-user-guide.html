2. How to use this guide

This is the student walkthrough for the intern portal. Match the pictures to the screen in front of you, then follow the steps. Watch the SitGuru videos on intern Help first, then use Definitions for field meanings.

The screenshots come from a SitGuru training intern login. You will see a practice name, school, and zeros. Your portal will show your name, school, tasks, and numbers.
  • Do not invent extra buttons. If a screen does not have a control, it is not part of your job yet.
  • Green buttons do the real work: send, save, submit. Only press them on your account when the work is ready.
  • SitGuru checks growth numbers. Your own count stays pending until they confirm it.

3. Log in

  1. Go to https://www.sitguru.com/intern.
  2. Sign in with the intern login SitGuru gave you. Do not share it. Do not let a roommate or classmate use it.
  3. If SitGuru has not added you to the program yet, you will see a message that this account is not assigned as an intern. Email SitGuru and wait. You cannot skip that step.

4. Onboarding (required first)

SitGuru unlocks Home, Work, Report, Metrics, and Growth workplace after four steps. Email confirmation goes to your email on file — SitGuru does not print a personal address on the submit screen.

  1. Access rules. Read the intern-safe duties. Confirm you understand them.
  2. Electronic signature. Read the intern agreement and sign with your legal name.
  3. Print, sign, upload. Print the page (or photograph a signed printout) and upload the PDF or photo. SitGuru keeps that copy as an extra policy record. The law already allows electronic signatures; the wet-ink page is SitGuru’s extra file, not a legal requirement.
  4. Submit. Confirmation is emailed to the address on your intern record. Then tap Open intern portal.
Do not paste SitGuru plans, passwords, customer names, or internal numbers into ChatGPT, school AI tools, or group chats. That is one of the access rules you accept here.

5. Home

Home is your weekly dashboard. The top cards show your school, program, term, and hours. The welcome block shows this week’s focus.

The four summary tiles are tappable:

  • This week — do your weekly check-in.
  • Open work — tasks still to do.
  • Metrics — numbers SitGuru has checked.
  • Social — posts you have logged.

Scroll for Growth workplace, the intern toolkit, and upcoming milestones. Use the green bar: Home · Calendar · Work · Report · Metrics.

6. Weekly check-in

Every week, tell SitGuru what you finished. Be specific — this writing goes into your Business Growth Report.

  1. Choose Contribution to your report (Market Analysis, Audience Strategy, Content System, Pet Parent Growth, and so on). That pick tells SitGuru which chapter this week belongs in.
  2. Fill in what you added, what you finished, what the numbers showed, what did not work, and what’s next. “What didn’t work” still counts — it becomes Lessons learned.
  3. Add Your number if you have one SitGuru can check (example: Pet Parent signups from your tracking link). Guessing stays pending forever.
  4. Tap Send this week to SitGuru.
This writing is your Business Growth Report in weekly pieces. Vague lines like “worked on social” give SitGuru nothing to verify. Be specific: who you reached, which tracking link, what you would change next week. Your school still owns credit. SitGuru letters are not a university grade.

7. Calendar

Calendar shows tasks, check-ins, and program milestones. Dots on a date mean something is scheduled.

Use Calendar to plan the week. Do the actual work on Work, log hours on Report, and send the weekly story from Home.

8. Work

Work is your assignment list. Filter Tasks, Social, or Campaigns.

Tasks

Open a task, attach proof, add the link, and send it to SitGuru.

  1. Attach PDFs, slides, screenshots, or drafts that prove the work. No customer names in the file.
  2. Paste a Link to completed work if you have one (Google Doc, Canva, published post after approval).
  3. Write What should SitGuru review? — what you did and where they should look. Empty send = nothing to review.
  4. Tap Send to SitGuru for review.

Social posts

Log every SitGuru post you draft or publish. Tag @SitGuruOfficial. Bookings stay on SitGuru. Never put a customer name or email in a caption.

Campaigns

A campaign is a named push with a tracking link. Create it before you post. SitGuru only counts growth from that link — not from a bare homepage URL.

9. Report and hours

Report is your Business Growth Report. It fills in from weekly check-ins, approved work, and hours you log here. Hours are not a separate Work screen — log them on Report.

  1. Enter Hours this week — the same honest total you would put on a school timesheet. SitGuru keeps this in the internship file. Your school still certifies credit hours.
  2. Tap Save to your report.
  3. Percent complete on Report rises when SitGuru accepts weekly work — not when you type. Measurable outcomes are SitGuru-checked numbers only.

10. Metrics

Metrics is where you send a number for SitGuru to check. Live people and social totals also show on Home. A letter (like I) is SitGuru’s current employer snapshot — it is not your university grade.

  1. Tap Send a number for SitGuru to check.
  2. Name the metric, enter the value, pick an approved source (analytics, tracking link, CRM, or another SitGuru-approved source), and add a source note so they can find it.
  3. Send it. Status stays Waiting until SitGuru confirms it. A letter (like I) is SitGuru’s employer snapshot — not your university grade.
No growth result is counted as attributable unless SitGuru can reasonably verify it through platform analytics, campaign tracking, referral codes, CRM records, or another approved source.

11. Intern toolkit

On Home, tap a toolkit card. Each tool has a colored bar and a ← Home back link.

Brand kit

  • Logo, brand green #0D5C3A, and handle @SitGuruOfficial.
  • White type on brand green. Never dark text on that green.
  • Do not put a university logo on SitGuru work unless SitGuru has recorded that the school said yes.
  • Rogue is the mascot. Bookings stay on SitGuru.

Tracking links

Create a unique link before you post. SitGuru only counts growth from that link.

Market snapshot

Market totals only — no names, emails, or payouts.

Events to share

Public pet events you can promote from intern Help or https://www.sitguru.com/events. Always use a tracking link. Open the event or copy the details. Do not add street addresses or partner emails that are not already on the public card. PA and NJ vendor research is intern Help only — SitGuru decides booth applications.

Social media (toolkit)

Same job as Work → Social, with official account shortcuts. SitGuru posts from official accounts unless they give you a channel.

12. Growth workplace

Growth workplace is on-the-project training for the SitGuru Market Growth Project. Write posts, tracking links, and campaigns here. SitGuru approves drafts before they go live. You do not get Admin HQ, passwords, or Pet Parent personal information.

Open it from the green Growth workplace button in the header, or from the Home card.

Create a post

Pick a type (Guru Spotlight, Event, Partner, PawPerks, Pet Parent, SitGuru Feature, or General Post). Write the title, channel, market, caption, destination, and date. After save, copy the tracking link, then publish in Canva / CapCut / Meta / TikTok. Do not publish until SitGuru approves.

Campaigns

Every real post gets a tracking link. Never send people to a bare SitGuru homepage.

Media

Drop Canva, CapCut, or Drive links here. SitGuru does not publish to Instagram for you — you still post in Meta and TikTok after approval.

13. Your page

Tap Your page in the header (or Edit your page on Home). This is your intern profile for SitGuru — not a public SitGuru.com guru listing.

You can update:

  • Preferred name and full name
  • Phone, student ID, and student email
  • Headline and about
  • LinkedIn
  • Page color
  • Profile photo (choose a file or take a photo)

School, program, and hours stay on the academic card at the top of Home. SitGuru reviews your internship work from this portal.

Keep phone numbers and student email in the form only. Do not put other people’s personal information on Your page, in captions, or in screenshots you share with classmates.

14. Rules that keep you intern-safe

  1. Use only the intern portal and Growth workplace. Do not try Admin HQ, Pet Parent records, passwords, payments, or Stripe/bank setup.
  2. Do not share this login. Report a lost device or a leaked password to SitGuru right away.
  3. SitGuru companions (Rogue, Taco, Scout, Delilah) and official intern Help videos are approved. Do not drop credentials, customer PII, or unpublished analytics into ChatGPT or other unapproved AI.
  4. Keep customer and Pet Parent names, emails, and phone numbers out of drafts, class slides, and portfolios. Use totals, not people.
  5. Do not count a result as “yours” until SitGuru verifies it.
  6. University logos stay off SitGuru work unless SitGuru recorded permission. SitGuru University certificates are not internship credit.
  7. Drafts wait for SitGuru. Do not publish, boost, or send SitGuru communications until they are approved.
  8. A cleaned-up portfolio version of your report needs SitGuru written approval first.
